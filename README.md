# 🎙️ VoiceByte AI

> **Just speak. I'll handle the rest.**

VoiceByte AI is an AI-powered voice-based food ordering assistant designed to make food discovery and ordering more natural and conversational.

Instead of searching through multiple apps and filters, users can simply speak about what they are craving. VoiceByte AI understands the request, explores relevant food options, compares ratings and reviews, checks delivery information, and provides personalized recommendations.

## ✨ Features

- 🎙️ **Voice-Based Interaction**
  - Interact with the application naturally using your voice.
  - Designed around a conversational food discovery experience.

- 👂 **Animated Listening Experience**
  - Visual feedback when the application is listening.
  - Helps users understand the current interaction state.

- 🧠 **AI-Powered Understanding**
  - Understands natural-language food requests and preferences.
  - Converts conversational requests into useful recommendations.

- 🔎 **Food Discovery**
  - Helps users discover relevant restaurants and food options based on their requirements.

- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews Comparison**
  - Compares available ratings and reviews to help users make better decisions.

- 🚚 **Delivery Information**
  - Provides delivery-related information as part of the recommendation process.

- ❤️ **Personalized Recommendations**
  - Tailors suggestions according to the user's preferences and conversation.

- 🛒 **Conversational Ordering Experience**
  - Designed to simplify the food-ordering journey through natural interaction.

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional food-ordering applications require users to repeatedly search, filter, compare restaurants, read reviews, and check delivery information.

This can make food discovery time-consuming, especially when users already know what they want but don't want to navigate through multiple screens.

## 💡 Solution

VoiceByte AI provides a conversational alternative.

Users can simply describe what they want, such as:

> "I'm craving something spicy and vegetarian."

The assistant can then understand the request, explore suitable options, compare relevant information, and present personalized recommendations.
